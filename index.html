How we work

Senior engineers, working directly with you

Custom financial software used to be expensive and slow: several developers, long schedules and a lot of coordination. That has changed. AI-assisted development is not a demo for us. It is how the projects below were built.

Every project is led by a senior engineer who works directly with you. Specialists are brought in when a project needs them.

  1. 01

    Discovery and design

    We sit with the people who do the work and learn the systems, the data, the workflows and the problems. Together we define the end state and agree how to get there.

  2. 02

    Rapid prototyping

    A working prototype arrives in days, so you can judge the approach before committing to the full build.

  3. 03

    Iterative development

    Once the approach is agreed, we set up the environment and the delivery process. Working software ships early and is refined through regular feedback from the people using it.

  4. 04

    Engineering oversight

    AI writes much of the code. An experienced engineer owns all of it: architecture, review, testing and deployment. Everything lives in secure, version-controlled repositories.

Three recent projects

Accounting system migration and data warehouse

About 350 hours. Estimated 1,500 to 2,000 the old way.

A fund moved from an in-house Geneva accounting system to its fund administrator's platform. We designed and built the data warehouse and migrated about 50 reports and data extracts.

The work took about 350 hours with AI-assisted development. On similar projects before that, the same scope took 1,500 to 2,000.

Delta Recon: AI-assisted reconciliation and operational controls

Built part-time in under three months

Delta Recon is our reconciliation and operational controls product: configurable reconciliation engines, automated data integrations, AI-assisted configuration and break analysis, exception management and operational dashboards.

It had been on the list for years. With AI-assisted development it was built part-time in under three months. deltarecon.ai

Investran report comparison and migration automation

In use at three large investment firms

An Investran upgrade means re-validating every report. This platform runs the reports on both versions, finds the differences and uses AI to classify each one and explain the differences.

Three large investment firms use it. It took most of the manual effort out of validating reports during their upgrades.
